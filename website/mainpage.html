Chapter Three

Nepali Cuisine

Nepalese cuisine is a rich blend of flavours, centred on staples served with spiced vegetables, pickles and curried meat. Favourites include momo and thukpa, while Himalayan regions bring their own unique ingredients. Simple yet flavourful, dishes are lifted with turmeric, cumin, timur pepper and fresh herbs.
