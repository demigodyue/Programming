Our Guides

Trek with people who live here

Every guide on our roster grew up in the valleys they walk you through. They carry wilderness first-aid certification, a satellite phone and a pulse oximeter, and they set the pace by how your body is handling the altitude — not by the itinerary on paper.

Porters are insured, weight-limited and equipped. Teahouses are family-run. The money you spend on the trail mostly stays on the trail.
