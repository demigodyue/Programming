Finding Us

Start in Kathmandu

Our office sits in Thamel, a ten-minute walk from Kathmandu Durbar Square and the departure point for every trip we run. Almost all routes begin here with a briefing and a kit check before the flight to Lukla or the drive to Pokhara.

Tribhuvan International is the only international airport in the country, twenty minutes from the office. We can arrange the pickup if you send us your flight number.

Planning a group trip?
