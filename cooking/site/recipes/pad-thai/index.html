Pad Thai

Rice noodles wok-tossed with shrimp, tofu and egg in a sweet-sour tamarind sauce, finished with crunchy peanuts, sprouts and lime.

Method

  1. 1

    Step 1: Soak the noodles in hot tap water for about 15 minutes until pliable but still firm at the centre. Drain.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Warm the tamarind, fish sauce and palm sugar in a small pan until the sugar dissolves. Taste — it should be sour, sweet and salty in equal measure.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Heat the oil in a wok over high heat. Fry the tofu until golden, 2–3 minutes, then add the shrimp, garlic, shallot and radish and stir-fry for 1 minute.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Push everything to one side, crack in the eggs and scramble until half set.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Add the noodles and sauce and toss for 2–3 minutes until the noodles are tender and have absorbed the sauce. Add a splash of water if they look dry.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Toss in half the bean sprouts and the chives for 30 seconds. Serve with peanuts, the remaining sprouts, lime wedges and chili flakes.

The story of this dish

Pad thai was promoted in the late 1930s and 1940s by Prime Minister Plaek Phibunsongkhram's government as a national dish, part of a campaign to build Thai identity and stretch rice supplies. Stir-fried rice noodles came from Chinese cooks, but the balance of tamarind, palm sugar and fish sauce is entirely Thai.
