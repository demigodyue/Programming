BibimbapBibimbap

Warm rice crowned with seasoned vegetables, garlicky beef and a fried egg, all mixed at the table with sweet-hot gochujang sauce.

Method

  1. 1

    Step 1: Toss the beef with the soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil and garlic and set aside while you prepare the vegetables.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Blanch the soybean sprouts in salted boiling water for 5 minutes, drain and season with a pinch of salt, a little garlic and 1 teaspoon sesame oil.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Blanch the spinach for 30 seconds, rinse under cold water, squeeze dry and season the same way.

  4. 4

    Step 4: In a little vegetable oil, stir-fry the carrot, zucchini and mushrooms separately for 1–2 minutes each with a pinch of salt, keeping them slightly crisp.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Stir-fry the beef over high heat for 2–3 minutes until just cooked. Mix all the gochujang sauce ingredients together.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Fry the eggs sunny side up. Divide the rice between bowls, arrange each topping in its own section, set an egg in the middle and add a spoonful of sauce. Mix everything together at the table.

The story of this dish

Bibimbap simply means “mixed rice.” Its roots are often traced to the Joseon era, possibly to the custom of mixing leftover dishes from ancestral rites into rice on the eve of the Lunar New Year. The city of Jeonju is famous for its version, and the sizzling stone-bowl dolsot bibimbap became popular in the twentieth century.
