Margherita PizzaMargherita Pizza

Neapolitan-style pizza with a puffy, blistered crust, raw crushed San Marzano tomatoes, fior di latte and fresh basil.

Method

  1. 1

    Step 1: Stir the yeast into the water, pour it into the flour and mix, then add the salt. Knead for 10 minutes until smooth and elastic.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Cover and let rise at room temperature for 1½–2 hours until doubled.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Divide into 4 balls of about 210 g, tucking the edges underneath to make them taut. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Meanwhile, heat the oven to its maximum (250–290 °C) with a pizza stone or steel inside for at least 45 minutes. Crush the tomatoes by hand with the salt — don't cook them.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Press a dough ball out from the centre with your fingertips, leaving a puffy 2 cm rim, then stretch it over your knuckles to about 25 cm. Don't use a rolling pin; it squeezes out the air.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Spread 3 tablespoons of tomato over the base, scatter a quarter of the mozzarella and drizzle with oil.

  7. 7

    Step 7: Slide onto the hot stone and bake for 6–8 minutes until the rim is puffed and leopard-spotted. Add the basil after baking and finish with a little more oil.

The story of this dish

Legend has it that in 1889 Neapolitan pizzaiolo Raffaele Esposito made a pizza in the colours of the Italian flag — red tomato, white mozzarella, green basil — for Queen Margherita of Savoy. Historians argue over the details, but pizza was already centuries old in Naples, and in 2017 UNESCO recognised the art of the Neapolitan pizzaiuolo.
