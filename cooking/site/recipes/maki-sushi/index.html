Maki Sushi Rolls

Seasoned sushi rice rolled in nori around salmon, cucumber and avocado — the best place to start making sushi at home.

Method

  1. 1

    Step 1: Rinse the rice in several changes of cold water until the water runs almost clear. Soak for 30 minutes, then drain well.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Cook the rice with the water in a rice cooker, or bring to a boil in a covered pot, turn the heat to the lowest setting for 12 minutes, then rest off the heat, still covered, for 10 minutes.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Warm the rice vinegar, sugar and salt until dissolved. Spread the hot rice in a wide bowl, pour the vinegar over a spatula and fold with cutting motions while fanning the rice until it cools to body temperature. Cover with a damp towel.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Cut the salmon into 1 cm-thick strips and the cucumber and avocado into long batons.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Lay a nori sheet shiny side down on a bamboo mat. With wet hands, spread one-sixth of the rice evenly over it, leaving a 2 cm strip bare along the top edge. Sprinkle with sesame seeds.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Line salmon, cucumber and avocado across the lower third. Lift the mat and roll away from you, tucking the filling in tightly, then press to seal the bare edge with a dab of water.

  7. 7

    Step 7: Cut each roll into 8 pieces with a sharp, wet knife, wiping the blade between cuts. Serve with soy sauce, pickled ginger and wasabi.

The story of this dish

Sushi began as narezushi — fish packed in fermenting rice to preserve it — a technique that reached Japan from Southeast Asia more than a thousand years ago. In 1820s Edo, Hanaya Yohei popularised nigiri: fresh fish on vinegared rice, served fast at street stalls. Rolled maki followed, and the sushi bar went global in the twentieth century.
