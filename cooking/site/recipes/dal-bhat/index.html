Dal BhatDal Bhat

Nepal's everyday meal: silky lentil dal finished with a sizzling garlic-cumin tempering, fluffy rice and a gently spiced potato-cauliflower tarkari.

Method

  1. 1

    Step 1: Rinse the lentils, then simmer with the water and turmeric for 25–30 minutes, stirring now and then, until completely soft. Whisk to a smooth soup and season with salt.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Rinse the rice, bring to a boil with the water, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 minutes. Rest, covered, for 5 minutes.

  3. 3

    Step 3: For the tarkari, heat the oil and fry the fenugreek seeds until dark, about 10 seconds. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, then the ginger, garlic and ground spices for 1 minute.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Add the tomatoes and cook for 3 minutes, then the potatoes, cauliflower, salt and water. Cover and simmer for 15–20 minutes until tender.

  5. 5

    Step 5: For the tempering, heat the ghee and fry the cumin seeds, chili, garlic and jimbu until the garlic is golden. Pour the sizzling ghee over the dal and cover for a minute to trap the aroma.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Serve rice with dal poured over or alongside, the tarkari and a pickle. In Nepal, second helpings are expected.

The story of this dish

“Dal bhat power, 24 hour” is a trekker's motto in Nepal, where lentils and rice are eaten twice a day in most homes, from the Terai lowlands to Everest base camp. Refills are customary: a good host keeps ladling until you insist you are full.
