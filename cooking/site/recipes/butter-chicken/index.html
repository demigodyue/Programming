Butter ChickenButter Chicken

Charred, yogurt-marinated chicken simmered in a velvety tomato, butter and cream sauce perfumed with fenugreek.

Method

  1. 1

    Step 1: Mix all the marinade ingredients with the chicken and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or up to overnight.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Spread the chicken on a rack set over a lined tray and grill under a very hot broiler for 10–12 minutes, turning once, until charred in spots. (Or sear in batches in a hot pan.)

  3. 3

    Step 3: For the sauce, heat the butter and oil and fry the ginger and garlic for 1 minute. Stir in the chili powder, then add the passata, drained cashews and water. Simmer for 15 minutes.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Blend the sauce until completely smooth — vent the blender lid, it's hot — and return it to the pan.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Add the salt, sugar, garam masala and the kasuri methi, crushed between your palms. Simmer for 5 minutes.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Stir in the chicken and cream and simmer for 5 minutes more. Finish with the butter and cilantro, and serve with naan or rice.

The story of this dish

Butter chicken, or murgh makhani, was born at Delhi's Moti Mahal restaurant, opened by Punjabi refugees after Partition in 1947. Its cooks reportedly tossed leftover tandoori chicken into a rich tomato, butter and cream gravy so it wouldn't dry out. The dish's invention is so prized that in 2024 two restaurant families took their dispute over it to the Delhi High Court.
