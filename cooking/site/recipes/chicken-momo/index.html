Chicken Momo

Juicy hand-pleated chicken dumplings, steamed and served with a smoky roasted-tomato and sesame achar.

Method

  1. 1

    Step 1: Mix the flour, salt, oil and water into a firm dough, adding a splash more water if it won't come together. Knead for 6–8 minutes until smooth, cover and rest for 30 minutes.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Combine all the filling ingredients, mixing until the meat turns slightly sticky — the butter and water keep the dumplings juicy. Chill until needed.

  3. 3

    Step 3: For the achar, char the tomatoes and chilies under a hot grill or in a dry pan until blistered, 8–10 minutes. Toast the sesame seeds until golden. Blend everything with the remaining achar ingredients until smooth.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Roll the dough into a log and cut into about 40 pieces. Roll each into an 8 cm circle with thinner edges and a slightly thicker centre.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Place a heaped teaspoon of filling in the centre and pleat the edges around it into a round purse, or fold into a pleated crescent.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Arrange the momos on an oiled steamer basket with space between them. Steam over rapidly boiling water for 10–12 minutes until the wrappers turn slightly translucent and the filling is cooked through. Serve hot with the achar.

The story of this dish

Momo came to Nepal with Newar traders who travelled the Himalayan routes to Lhasa and adapted Tibetan dumplings to local spices. Today Kathmandu's momos — filled with buffalo, chicken or vegetables and dunked in sesame-tomato achar — are Nepal's favourite snack, sold everywhere from street carts to wedding feasts.
