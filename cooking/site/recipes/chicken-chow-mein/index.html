Chicken Chow MeinChicken Chow Mein

Springy egg noodles stir-fried over high heat with chicken, cabbage and bean sprouts in a savoury oyster-soy sauce.

Method

  1. 1

    Step 1: Toss the chicken with the soy sauce, cornstarch and Shaoxing wine and leave for 10 minutes.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Cook the noodles in boiling water according to the packet, usually 1–2 minutes, until just tender. Rinse under cold water, drain very well and toss with 1 teaspoon of oil.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Mix all the sauce ingredients together.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Heat a wok until smoking, add 1 tablespoon oil and stir-fry the chicken for 2–3 minutes until cooked through. Set aside.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Add another tablespoon of oil, then the garlic, carrot and cabbage, and stir-fry for 1–2 minutes.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Add the remaining oil and the noodles. Spread them out and leave for a minute to crisp slightly, then toss. Add the sauce and chicken and toss for 1–2 minutes until evenly coloured.

  7. 7

    Step 7: Add the bean sprouts and green onions, toss for 30 seconds and serve straight away.

The story of this dish

Chow mein — chāu-mihn, “stir-fried noodles” in Cantonese — comes from Taishan in Guangdong, home of many of the first Chinese immigrants to the United States in the 1800s. It became one of the first Chinese dishes to win over America, and it is now cooked in countless styles, from crispy Hong Kong pan-fried noodles to soft Caribbean versions.
